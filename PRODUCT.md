# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two equal audiences: potential clients (e-commerce brands, SMBs seeking Google Ads, GA4, AI automation) and employers/recruiters hiring for AI engineering or UX roles. Both arrive cold — neither has context. Both need to leave with a clear picture of capability depth, not just vibes.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio site that functions as a capability proof. Idris-Michael Bakare is an AI Integrator and Agentic Developer running Severus Connects, a London-based performance marketing agency. The site must communicate both identities simultaneously: the technical builder (AI systems, agents, automations) and the agency operator (Google Ads, GA4, n8n workflows, client results). Success means a visitor understands the full stack — personal craft plus agency output — without needing to ask follow-up questions.

## Brand Personality

Precise, earned, cinematic. Three words: technical confidence, no noise.

The voice is expert-quiet: it doesn't need to shout because the work speaks. "Agentic Dev" is a real identity, not a buzzword costume. The agency arm (Severus Connects) carries its own gold identity — distinct from the personal emerald — but both live on the same site without fighting.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS portfolio (floating cards, gradient hero, "I build things" headline)
- Freelancer marketplace profiles (Fiverr/Upwork aesthetic, rate cards front and centre)
- Over-animated agency sites that obscure the actual work
- Dark + neon crypto/web3 aesthetic
- "AI agency" template sites with robot icons and blue gradients

## Design Principles

1. **Show the system, not just the output.** Agents, CLAUDE.md rules, skill stacks — these are differentiators. Surface the architecture, not just the results.
2. **Two identities, one coherent site.** Personal (emerald) and agency (gold) coexist without clash. Colour is the separator, not layout.
3. **Credibility through specificity.** ROAS 0.9× → 2.4×, not "improved performance." Agent names, not "AI tools."
4. **No redundancy.** Each section earns its place. No restated headings, no section intros that repeat what the nav already said.
5. **The work is the hero.** Architecture video, flow visualiser, live dashboards — real artefacts over marketing copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Sufficient contrast on both black and deep-navy surfaces.
