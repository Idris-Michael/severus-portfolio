§ 1 — Delivery Timeline
The Service Provider shall deliver all work within 14 calendar days of receipt of brief. Failure to meet this deadline shall result in a penalty of 5% of total contract value per day.
● HIGH RISK
§ 2 — Termination Rights
The Client reserves the right to terminate this agreement without notice if deliverables are deemed unsatisfactory at the Client's sole discretion. No appeal mechanism is provided.
● HIGH RISK
§ 3 — Penalty Cap
Accumulated penalties shall not exceed 50% of total contract value. The penalty applies regardless of cause, including force majeure events.
● MEDIUM RISK
§ 4 — Intellectual Property
All work product, including source code, designs, and documentation, shall be considered work-for-hire and immediately assigned to the Client upon delivery or payment, whichever occurs first.
● LOW RISK
§ 5 — Dispute Resolution
Any disputes arising from this agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration under the rules of the London Court of International Arbitration within 60 days of notice.
● ACCEPTABLE
