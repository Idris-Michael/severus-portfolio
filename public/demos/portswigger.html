Dual-LLM Contract Review.

Submitted to PortSwigger's AI Legal Pioneer competition. Claude drafts the risk analysis, Gemini cross-checks for bias. Two independent models reach consensus — surfacing risks neither would catch alone.

Contract Input
Sample Contract Clause
"The Service Provider shall deliver all work within 14 calendar days of receipt of brief. Failure to meet this deadline shall result in a penalty of 5% of total contract value per day, up to a maximum of 50% of the total contract value. The Client reserves the right to terminate this agreement without notice if deliverables are deemed unsatisfactory at the Client's sole discretion."
Dual-LLM Analysis
Claude (Anthropic)
Primary Risk Drafter
3 HIGH risk clauses identified.

The 5%/day penalty with no force majeure carve-out creates existential financial exposure. The "sole discretion" termination clause removes all objective standards — recommend binding arbitration clause.

Suggest: cap penalties at 15%, add 5-day cure period before termination, define "satisfactory" with measurable KPIs.
Gemini (Google)
Bias Cross-Check
Claude's analysis confirmed. No pro-client bias detected.

Additional flag: "14 calendar days" includes weekends and holidays — effectively 10 working days. Recommend "14 business days" or explicit exclusion of public holidays.

Termination clause lacks notice period — recommend minimum 48h written notice before activation.
