Video Flywheel Pipeline.

Three open-source tools integrated into Hermes OS to create a complete content and proof-of-work video pipeline — OpenScreen for Upwork demos, Voicebox for emotion-cloned narration, OpenShorts for viral crop automation.

Three Tools, One Pipeline
🖥️
OpenScreen
Phase 1 · Week 1–2
Records and auto-annotates GA4 dashboard walkthroughs — click highlights, auto-zoom on key metrics, exports 16:9 MP4 for Upwork bid attachments.
+30% Upwork win rate → +£300–600/mo
🎙️
Voicebox
Phase 2 · Week 3–4
Local voice cloning via Kokoro 82M model. 7 TTS engines, emotion tags, zero cost. One 10-second sample clones the voice for all future reel narration.
-$0.20/mo TTS cost · +15% Reel engagement
✂️
OpenShorts
Phase 3 · Week 5–6
Auto-crops long-form content into 9:16 viral shorts using Gemini 3.5 Flash for speaker detection. FastAPI + FFmpeg. ~$0.01/video.
3× content velocity for @mikeb.io
